I want to build a really simple web app that allows the user to practice their handwriting or just draw when they want to calm themselves down or entertain a little bit.

The use case might be that they want to draw a little bit or try writing a little bit when they're commuting or when they have a little bit of spare time and want to entertain. For example, they can take pictures of a slogan looks beautiful in front of them, and then the app can generate different types of fonts, colors, or brushes, or mimic different types of painting, like watercolor, oil painting, drawing, and lines, to give the user the feel of designing the slogan they just saw.

Writing for example, in Chinese, there are different ways they want to practice their writing, like practicing writing with a poem from search or from screenshot/photo. They can start mirroring the writing in different fonts and from different famous hand-writing Calligrapher.
